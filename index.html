      Instructions

      How to Play Spyfall

      Welcome to Spyfall! Gather your friends and get ready for a game of mystery, bluffing, and clever deduction. Here’s how you can dive into the fun:

      Setting Up the Game

      1. Open the Game Setup: This is your control center where you can tailor the game settings.
      2. Add Players: Enter the names of all players participating. You need at least 3 players; 3-8 is ideal.
      3. Set Number of Spies: Decide how many spies you want in the game. More spies increase complexity and fun!
      4. Manage Places: Tap on ‘Manage Places’ to view, add, or delete the list of possible locations. Each non-spy player will receive one of these locations.
      5. Start the Game: Once settings are configured, hit ‘Start Game’ to begin the mystery!

      Playing the Game

      1. Card Assignment: Pass the phone around. Each player will tap to receive a card — you're either a Spy or a Non-Spy. If you're a Non-Spy, you will see a location. Every Non-Spy will get the same location! If you're a Spy, your card will not reveal a location, but tell you that you are a Spy.
      2. Discussion Round: Once everyone has seen their card, the round timer starts (set its length or turn it off under ‘Round Timer’ in the Game Setup). Discuss among yourselves to deduce who the spies are. Players should ask each other questions about the location to figure out who doesn't belong, while spies bluff to blend in.
      3. Reveal Spies: When the timer runs out, or earlier if you're ready, tap the ‘Reveal Spies’ button. Spies are never revealed automatically.
      4. Ending the Game: After the spies are revealed, you can discuss how the round went and then press ‘End Game’ to return to the Game Setup for another round.

      Enjoy playing Spyfall!
